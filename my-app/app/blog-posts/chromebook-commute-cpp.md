# Why I Use an Old Chromebook for C++ Practice

I picked up an old Chromebook from Facebook Marketplace to practice C++ on my commute.

![My Chromebook](/blog-images/laptop.jpg)

My aim was to have some low cost laptop where I could focus on refining my skillset, something where I can't get distrated.

![My LC](/blog-images/leetcode_screenshot.png)

## Why This Setup Worked

Having just a chromebook with a linux VM was really fun, I like the idea of tech minimalist. Since its low powered and a bit dated I couldn't install tools like claude code or codex (I atleast didn't try to...). The whole goal here was to just use vim, gcc, and some other tools to really learn C++ and System fundamentals. I also did a lot of LC during this time.

## Commute Training

During this time my commut was about 50 minutes by train, I typically spent the mornings doing LC, and the evenings reading a dense textbook. 

## Tech Minimalism

I've noticed when tech is more specialized it's more appreciated. Digital single purpose cameras make taking photos seem more authentic. Really makes you appreciate the craft, thats how I threat my chrome book. 

## Final Takeaway

I highly recommend trying a small, older laptop for focused training.

Tech minimalizing plus daily practice is a real multiplier, especially if you are trying to learn a new language.
